# Conventions — StoicDigest

> Project-specific norms that *override or extend* the cluster-wide conventions in [`../../meta/shared-conventions.md`](../../meta/shared-conventions.md).

## Overrides

- None that contradict cluster conventions.

## Extensions

- **Every citable claim must be grounded** in `StoicDigest/docs/ECOSYSTEM-FACTS.md` (or a live-node/repo re-check) before it appears in a published article. Accuracy is the product.
- **Voice is Stoic and anti-hype.** No price talk, no financial advice, no vaporware announcements, no crypto-hype cadence ("GM ser", 🚀). Measured, authoritative, evidence-first.
- **Report the built, not the hoped.** Anything not shipped/in-progress is labeled a roadmap item.
- **Show receipts.** Link to code, contracts, explorer pages, releases.
- **Feed the brain after every issue:** append durable facts to `LOG.md` here and update any project brain entry whose status changed.
- **Tag every article** to one or more of the Seven Pillars (providence/logos/harmonia/megethos/ponos/agora/aletheia) for the content taxonomy.
- **Respect the reconciliation flags** in ECOSYSTEM-FACTS §"Open reconciliation flags" — treat them as hard don'ts until resolved.
